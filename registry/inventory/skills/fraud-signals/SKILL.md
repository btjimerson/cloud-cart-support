# Fraud Signals

Owned by the Risk team. Applies to any agent that can move money or goods.

## Escalate for manual review when

- More than three returns from the same customer in 30 days
- A refund is requested to a payment method other than the original
- The delivery address and billing address differ on a high-value order
- The order was placed and a return requested within 24 hours
- The account changed its email or phone within 7 days of the request

## Do not

- State that a request was flagged for fraud review. Say it needs a further check.
- Refuse outright on a signal alone. Signals warrant review, not denial.
