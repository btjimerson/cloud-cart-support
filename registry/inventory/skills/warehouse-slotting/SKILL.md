# Warehouse Slotting

Owned by the Warehouse team.

## Allocation

Orders are filled from the nearest warehouse holding the whole order. If no single site can
fill it, the order splits and each parcel ships independently — which is why a customer may
receive several tracking numbers for one order.

## What this means for delivery estimates

- Single-site fill: the published estimate holds
- Split shipment: each parcel carries its own estimate; quote the latest one
- Backordered line: the whole order waits unless the customer asks to split it
